## The evaluation grant

**This repository asks to be checked, and a claim that cannot be checked is
the thing it was built to refuse.** The `README` instructs a reader to clone,
build and run the harness against the engine. Under all rights reserved that
instruction is broader than the grant behind it, which is the exact fault
this repository names elsewhere: an invitation whose warrant was never
crossed.

So the warrant is crossed here, and no wider than it has to be.

> **You may clone, build, run, read and test this repository, and quote its
> output, for the purpose of evaluating, reviewing, reproducing or
> criticising its claims.** This permission is granted to anyone, is not
> transferable, is not sublicensable, and conveys no right to use this code
> or any part of it in another work, product or service.

What this does not give: the right to incorporate the code anywhere, to
distribute modified copies, or to build a product on it. Those remain
undecided and are available on request.

What it does give is the only thing the evidence needs: that anybody may
re-run the attacks and say in public whether they hold.

Quoting for criticism, review, scholarship or news reporting is generally

**ND does not reach the idea.** Copyright covers this expression of the
Sentinel Principle, not the principle. Anyone may read it, disagree with it,
and build their own implementation of it; that is the point of publishing it
and nothing here is intended to discourage it. What ND withholds is the
redistribution of altered copies of *these documents*, which is a different
thing and is withheld so that a modified declaration cannot circulate under
this repository's name.

Creative Commons licences are **irrevocable**. Distribution can be stopped,
but the grant cannot be withdrawn from anyone who already received the
material under it. That is understood and intended.

## The declarations are dated evidence, not properties

`DECLARATION.md` and `DECLARATION-0005.md` are generated by a run, on one
machine, on one date, against one build. They are evidence about that run and
no other. Quoting them is free under the terms above; quoting them as a
standing property of this software, or of anything built from it, is a
misattribution the licence does not reach and `INTENT.md` asks you not to
make.
