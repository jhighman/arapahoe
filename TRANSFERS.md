# What this repository owes, and to whom

*Opened 2026-09-26. Kept in the shape of the engine's `BENCH-TRANSFERS.md`:
findings adopted by citation, with status, and the return traffic in the
same file.*

The one disclosure that governs every row: the bench, the engine, the
blueprint and this repository are the work of one pair of authors and their
two assistants. Agreement between them is worth what the engine's
`BENCH-TRANSFERS.md` §3 says such agreement is worth.

## 1 · The six questions — **OPEN, all six**

Each was read against the blueprint in the bench's `ARAPAHOE-READING.md`
and then built and attacked there, in the bench's idiom. None has been
asked of ARAPAHOE itself. A build here answers each by being attacked, not
by argument, and the answer is a row here with the result in.

| # | Question | Bench guard | What the build owes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Does any surface wear the refused identifier? | `vocabulary.py` | A guard in the build's own language. **Built for the target**: the harness's Q1 scans both crates on every run. A build in a further language owes its own |
| 2 | Does a refused proposal leave a row? | `envelope.py` | Handed the 99-then-1 traffic beside the 1-at-first traffic, the record distinguishes the two authors |
| 3 | Does a transition pay for what it reaches? | `weight.py` | Reach classified by a non-author; price in distinct voices; the author's own endorsement counts for nothing |
| 4 | Is the Canon the Sentinel read derived, or believed? | `canon.py` | A forged reading in the Sentinel's name buys nothing; a Canon furnished after entry moves nothing |
| 5 | Is the certificate bound to the Sentinel? | (reading only; no guard) | The forgery case the blueprint's tests do not cover |
| 6 | What does a released action leave live when the releaser dies? | `outlives.py` | A term on every release, or the record saying there is none; a reconciler that shares no fate |

## 2 · ABE, and the target's declaration — **TARGET RUN; ARAPAHOE NOT RUN**

Nothing here is a declaration by ARAPAHOE, which does not exist. It is the
declaration of the Sentinel-shaped target of `decisions/0002`, run by the
harness across a pipe, and its purpose is to show that the attacks bite,
not that anything conforms.

**Against the six questions** (`DECLARATION.md`, regenerated by
`cargo run -p harness`): Q1 through Q6 PASS, each with its evidence in the
row. The one number worth carrying: the same traffic that left the silent
envelope unable to tell two authors apart leaves this one reading
`stubborn: 100 attempts, 99 refusals; lucky: 1 attempt, 0 refusals`.

**Against ABE v0.1**, in the sense a harness can give the tests across a
pipe, with the standard's own numbering:

| Test | Result | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| T5 · the guard cannot be removed by the actor it constrains | **FAIL, expected** | Two processes, one OS user. The harness opened the ledger file and appended an Accept row; it succeeded. On replay the derivation refused to believe the row (`void=1`), which is the bench's "not credulous" and is not custody |
| T7 · the record cannot be amended, and attempts are recorded | PASS | No message in the envelope amends or deletes; each attempt is a knock, and a knock is a row. This is the half the engine and the ABE reference implementation fail |
| T8 · silence is not consent | PASS | Three-valued, and the third value lands as its own act (`undecided`), kept apart from `decline` |

| T1, T2, T3, T4, T6 | NOT RUN | Reported as not run, never folded into pass |

**The harness was checked the only way a self-written suite can be.** Both
sides were written by one process. So the target was broken deliberately,
four ways, and the harness had to go red:

| Mutation | What turned red | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Refusals stop being rows (the envelope's knock appends nothing) | T7 | Q5 stayed green because a forged release is knocked by a different path; recorded, not fixed |
| The Canon is believed as it stands now, not derived at entry | **nothing, on the first run** — then Q4, after Q4 was strengthened | The first Q4 only read `accepted`, which is false under both the deriver and the believer because no voice had endorsed. The attack that tells them apart is to furnish the Canon after entry and then endorse, timed to match. The first Q4 measured its author's imagination; the second measures the target |

| Release before append | not mutated | The harness cannot observe the order of two writes across a pipe; the ordering is held by reading the code, and is stated as a limit in 0002 |

The second row is the finding of the day on this side, and it is against the
harness: a green suite on the first run was one weak assertion away from
certifying a believer as a deriver. It is kept here in the column marked
against the author, per the bench's `axiom_zero.py` reason.

## 4 · Owed

- **Reach named by someone other than the author.** The target takes a
  proposal's reach from the proposer, and the harness's Q3 never tests
  otherwise, so `DECLARATION.md` reads Q3 PASS against a row in §1 that
  promises more than the target does. The bench's `weight.py` refutes
  exactly this: "a reach an author can set for itself." Found by the game's
  streaks district on 2026-09-26, where the operator files a nudge to a
  phone as reaching only the record and pays two voices for the world. A
  fix is a decision before it is code: who names reach, and where that row
  comes from.
- The blueprint itself, dated, with its acceptance tests.
- The three decisions of 0001, each as a numbered file in `decisions/`.
- A run of the harness against a build that is not the target, by hands
  that did not write the harness.
- Ordering observed rather than read: a way for something outside the
  process to see that the append preceded the release.
