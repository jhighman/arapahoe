# What this repository owes, and to whom

*Opened 2026-09-26. Kept in the shape of the engine's `BENCH-TRANSFERS.md`:
findings adopted by citation, with status, and the return traffic in the
same file.*

The one disclosure that governs every row: the bench, the engine, the
blueprint and this repository are the work of one pair of authors and their
two assistants. Agreement between them is worth what the engine's
`BENCH-TRANSFERS.md` §3 says such agreement is worth.

## 1 · The six questions — **OPEN, all six**

Each was read against the blueprint in the bench's `ARAPAHOE-READING.md`
and then built and attacked there, in the bench's idiom. None has been
asked of ARAPAHOE itself. A build here answers each by being attacked, not
by argument, and the answer is a row here with the result in.

| # | Question | Bench guard | What the build owes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Does any surface wear the refused identifier? | `vocabulary.py` | A guard in the build's own language; until then, reported as unenforced |
| 2 | Does a refused proposal leave a row? | `envelope.py` | Handed the 99-then-1 traffic beside the 1-at-first traffic, the record distinguishes the two authors |
| 3 | Does a transition pay for what it reaches? | `weight.py` | Reach classified by a non-author; price in distinct voices; the author's own endorsement counts for nothing |
| 4 | Is the Canon the Sentinel read derived, or believed? | `canon.py` | A forged reading in the Sentinel's name buys nothing; a Canon furnished after entry moves nothing |
| 5 | Is the certificate bound to the Sentinel? | (reading only; no guard) | The forgery case the blueprint's tests do not cover |
| 6 | What does a released action leave live when the releaser dies? | `outlives.py` | A term on every release, or the record saying there is none; a reconciler that shares no fate |

decision (0001, open item 1) is what T5 measures, so T5's result is the
first number this file will carry.
